How to play

  1. You start with 3 words in your web, not connected to each other yet.
  2. Tap any word bubble. Every real word that's just one letter different will show up below it as a choice.
  3. Tap a choice to add it to your web, connected to the bubble you tapped.
  4. Keep going, tapping any bubble in your growing web, until all 3 starting words are linked into a single web.
  5. The fewer extra words you need, the better your score. Par is the fewest possible — matching it is a perfect score, and there's no going back once a word is placed.

A worked example

Say your 3 words are HOUSE, GOOSE, and WORSE. One way to connect them:

HOUSE → HORSE → WORSE

HOUSE → MOUSE → MOOSE → GOOSE

Each arrow changes exactly one letter. That's 5 extra connections in total — Word Web will tell you the real par for your puzzle, which might beat that.
